# Privacy Policy for MySumsel

**Last Updated: April 28, 2026**

MySumsel ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Location Information
To provide you with nearby utility information (such as hospitals, police stations, and money changers), the App requests access to your device's precise location (GPS and network-based). This information is sent to our servers via encrypted headers (`X-Latitude` and `X-Longitude`) to calculate and display the nearest services.

### 1.2 Device Information
We may collect standard device information such as your device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers to ensure the App performs correctly and for troubleshooting purposes.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
* Provide and maintain the App's core functionality.
* Show nearby essential services based on your current location.
* Improve the user experience and App performance.
* Send notifications regarding local events (if opted-in).

## 3. Data Storage and Security
We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your location data is processed in real-time to provide search results and is not stored permanently on our servers unless specifically required for a feature you explicitly use.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
* **Google Play Services**: Used for App distribution and performance monitoring.
* **Cloudinary**: Used for hosting and delivering images within the App.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Your Consent
By using our App, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
